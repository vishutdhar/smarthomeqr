Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Summary: SmartHomeQR runs no analytics, shows no ads, and never sells your data or shares it beyond the storage service you choose. On iOS your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. On Android it stays on your phone unless you turn on optional cloud sync, which stores it in a Google Firebase database for this app.

Introduction

SmartHomeQR ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS and Android applications.

Data Collection

We run no analytics and take nothing for ourselves. On iOS, SmartHomeQR operates entirely on your device and your personal iCloud account; we have no servers there and no way to access your information. On Android, everything stays on your phone unless you turn on the optional Cloud Sync setting. When Cloud Sync is on, your homes, rooms, devices, QR images and setup codes are stored in a Google Firebase database operated for this app, tied to your Google sign-in, and used only to sync your devices. They are not yet end to end encrypted; a future update will add that. We do not look at, analyze, or share this data.

Data Storage

All data you create in SmartHomeQR is stored:

  • Locally on your device - Your homes, rooms, devices, and QR code images are stored in your device's local storage.
  • iOS, in your iCloud account - If you have iCloud enabled, your data syncs privately through Apple's iCloud service to your other devices. This sync is encrypted and managed entirely by Apple.
  • Android, in Firebase when Cloud Sync is on - Cloud Sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your data is copied to a Google Firebase database for this app so your devices can sync. Turning it off stops syncing immediately and keeps your local data.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or statistics
  • Device identifiers
  • Your QR codes and setup codes never leave your device unless you enable iCloud (iOS) or Cloud Sync (Android)
  • Crash reports (unless you choose to share with Apple)

Third-Party Services

SmartHomeQR does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external services used are Apple's iCloud (iOS, optional) and Google Firebase (Android, only when you enable Cloud Sync; see Firebase privacy). iCloud is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera and Photo Library

SmartHomeQR requests access to your camera and photo library solely to capture or import QR code images. These images are stored on your device, in your iCloud account (iOS), or in Firebase when Android Cloud Sync is on. We do not transmit them anywhere else.

Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual items within the app at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Manage iCloud data through your device's Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud
  • On Android, turn off Cloud Sync at any time; deleting a device in the app also deletes its cloud copy, and you can email us to have all your cloud data removed

Children's Privacy

SmartHomeQR does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The only data the app stores is the vault a user builds themselves, on their own device or in the storage service they choose.

Changes to This Policy
