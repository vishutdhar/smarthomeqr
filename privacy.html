Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Summary: SmartHomeQR runs no analytics of its own, shows no ads, and never sells your data. Your codes and photos go nowhere beyond the storage service you choose. On iOS your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud. On Android it stays on your phone; the current Android release offers no cloud sync. The one exception is technical: the Android app reads QR codes and labels on the phone with Google ML Kit, whose software reports diagnostic data about each scan (device model, OS version, app version, a per-installation identifier, timing and error codes) to Google. That diagnostic data never includes your codes or photos.

Introduction

SmartHomeQR ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our iOS and Android applications.

Data Collection

We run no analytics of our own and take nothing for ourselves. On iOS, SmartHomeQR operates entirely on your device and your personal iCloud account; we have no servers there and no way to access your information. On Android, your vault stays on your phone; the only thing that leaves it is the ML Kit scan diagnostics described under Third-Party Services. The current Android release does not offer Cloud Sync. When a future release adds it, it will be off by default; if you turn it on, your homes, rooms, devices, QR images and setup codes will be stored in a Google Firebase database operated for this app, tied to your Google sign-in, and used only to sync your devices. They will not be end to end encrypted at first; a later update will add that. We do not look at, analyze, or share this data.

Data Storage

All data you create in SmartHomeQR is stored:

  • Locally on your device - Your homes, rooms, devices, and QR code images are stored in your device's local storage.
  • iOS, in your iCloud account - If you have iCloud enabled, your data syncs privately through Apple's iCloud service to your other devices. This sync is encrypted and managed entirely by Apple.
  • Android, in Firebase when Cloud Sync is on - not offered in the current Android release. When a future release adds Cloud Sync it will be off by default; if you turn it on, your data is copied to a Google Firebase database for this app so your devices can sync. Turning it off stops syncing immediately and keeps your local data.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or statistics of our own (Google's ML Kit software on Android reports scan diagnostics and usage events to Google, described below)
  • Advertising identifiers, or anything that identifies you; on Android the only identifier that leaves the phone is the per-installation one ML Kit sends to Google, described below
  • Your QR codes and setup codes never leave your device unless you enable iCloud (iOS) or, in a future Android release that offers it, Cloud Sync
  • Crash reports (unless you choose to share them with Apple or Google through your device's diagnostics settings, in which case they reach us through the App Store or Google Play)

Third-Party Services

SmartHomeQR does not integrate with any advertising or tracking services and adds no analytics of its own. The external services involved are Apple's iCloud (iOS, optional); Google ML Kit on Android, which performs barcode and text recognition on your phone and whose software sends Google diagnostic data about each scan (device model, OS version, app version, a per-installation identifier that Google says is not intended to uniquely identify a user or physical device, timing and error codes), described in Google's ML Kit disclosure, and never your codes or photos; and Google Firebase (Android, only in a future release and only when you enable Cloud Sync; see Firebase privacy). iCloud is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Camera and Photo Library

SmartHomeQR requests access to your camera and photo library solely to capture or import QR code images. These images are stored on your device, in your iCloud account (iOS), or, once a future Android release offers Cloud Sync and you turn it on, in Firebase. We do not transmit them anywhere else.

Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual items within the app at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Manage iCloud data through your device's Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud
  • On Android, once Cloud Sync is offered, turn it off at any time; deleting a device in the app also deletes its cloud copy, and you can email us to have all your cloud data removed

Children's Privacy

SmartHomeQR does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The only data the app stores is the vault a user builds themselves, on their own device or in the storage service they choose.

Changes to This Policy
