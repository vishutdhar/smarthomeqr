Guides

Where the setup code is printed, brand by brand

One line per brand, from the maker's own support pages where they exist. Each name opens the full page with the sources.

Checked against the makers' own pages on 3 November 2026.

Know what you are looking for

HomeKit
Eight digits as XXX-XX-XXX beside a small house mark, usually with a QR code.
Matter
A QR code with the Matter mark and an 11 digit code, usually printed as XXXX-XXX-XXXX. Works at first setup and after a reset; a second ecosystem gets a code from the first app instead.
Z Wave
Groups of five digits. The first group, often underlined, is the PIN typed in during inclusion.

Brand by brand

Amazon Echo and Alexa
None on the Echo. The accessory's code, typed under Try Numeric Code Instead.
Aqara
Camera E1 code in the Aqara Home app; for other products, owners report the device, the box or the manual.
August
Inside the front battery cover, on the serial number label; press the August logo to open it.
Eve
Sticker on the accessory and in the quick start guide; after the Matter upgrade, in the Eve app.
Google Nest
From Google Home: Linked Matter apps and services, a temporary code.
Govee
On the product, and on the Device Settings page in the Govee Home app.
HomePod and Apple TV
None. HomePod pairs by proximity; Apple TV joins from its settings.
IKEA DIRIGERA
Matter QR on the yellow cover in the box; the hub's HomeKit code in the IKEA Home smart app.
Kwikset
Matter QR on the lock after converting it in the Kwikset app; first platform only.
LIFX
Below the serial number, marked HK:; Beam and Lightstrip on the controller; Matter lights beside the Matter mark.
Level
Owner reports: back of the quick start guide (Bolt, Lock Plus), underside of the original Level Lock.
Leviton Decora Smart
Lower right corner of the device behind the wall plate; after the Matter update, in the My Leviton app.
Lutron Caseta
Bottom of the Smart Hub. Dimmers and switches have no code.
Meross
In the Meross app under Device Settings, Device Info, HomeKit setup code.
Nanoleaf
Back of the controller or bulb base, and on the welcome card.
Philips Hue
Matter bulbs: on the bulb and in the installation guide. With a bridge: from the Hue app.
Ring
Ring QR and 5 digit code on the back, for the Ring app. No HomeKit code; no Matter support found.
SONOFF
In the quick guide; and in the eWeLink app under Device Settings.
Schlage
Inside the interior assembly, under the battery cover; backup label on the quick start guide.
Sengled
On the quick start guide.
SmartThings
Matter digits under Enter setup code; Z Wave PIN is the first five DSK digits; hub QR on the hub.
SwitchBot
Hub 2: side or back; and in the SwitchBot app under Matter Setup.
TP-Link Tapo and Kasa
Side of a plug, neck of a bulb, beside a switch button; backup sticker in the box; in the app under Device Info.
Tuya and Smart Life
Varies by brand; the app pairs with the printed code and shares with a temporary one.
Wemo
Back of the device and in the quick install guide. New HomeKit setups ended January 31, 2026.
Yale
Side of the battery compartment under the cover; setup QR also on the quick start guide.
ecobee
On the thermostat's screen under Main Menu, General, Settings, HomeKit. No sticker.
eufy
Bottom of HomeBase 2; or HomeBase Settings, HomeKit Setup in the eufy app.

Questions

My brand is not here
Write to support@freedom-terminal.com with the product name and a photo of its label, and it will be added with its source.
The sticker is gone
See Lost your HomeKit code or Lost your Matter pairing code. Matter devices still paired somewhere are usually recoverable; HomeKit codes usually are not.
